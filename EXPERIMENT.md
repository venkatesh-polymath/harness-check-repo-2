## Study spec
- goal: Measure whether Deep Ensemble (K=5) or MC-Dropout (T=20) yields a superior Pareto frontier of pseudo-label precision vs. coverage on CIFAR-10 at exactly 50 and 100 labeled examples per class, using a single round of pseudo-labeling with threshold sweep.
- scientific (measure): ['uncertainty_method: {deep_ensemble_K5, mc_dropout_T20, msp_single_model [baseline]}', 'labels_per_class: {50, 100}', 'pseudo_label_confidence_threshold: sweep [0.50, 0.55, …, 0.99] (20 points, uniform grid)']
- nuisance (tune per arm): ['dropout_rate (MC-Dropout arm only): tuned per arm via held-out ECE on the labeled val split; Deep Ensemble arm uses p=0.0', 'per-member init seed offset (Deep Ensemble arm only): {0,1,2,3,4} added to global seed so members diverge; MC-Dropout uses single init', 'learning_rate: independently grid-searched per arm on labeled val split ({1e-2, 3e-3, 1e-3})', 'weight_decay: independently grid-searched per arm ({1e-3, 5e-4, 1e-4})']
- fixed (hold constant): ['architecture: ResNet-8 (CIFAR-variant, ~6 layers, no pre-training) — caveat: results may not transfer to larger nets', 'dataset: CIFAR-10 (50 k train / 10 k test, standard split)', 'optimizer: SGD + cosine annealing LR schedule, momentum=0.9', 'batch_size: 64', 'training_epochs: 200 (validated by overfit-single-batch sanity check in ≤200 steps)', 'global_random_seed: 42 (torch, numpy, python random — reproducibility verified by bit-identical loss-curve sanity check)', 'labeled_split_strategy: stratified random, 5 independent draws per label count condition', 'unlabeled_pool: all remaining training images after labeled draw (no filtering)', 'pseudo-label round: single round only (no iterative re-training)', 'data_augmentation: RandomHorizontalFlip + RandomCrop(32, padding=4) — identical across all arms', 'normalization: CIFAR-10 per-channel mean/std (μ=[0.4914,0.4822,0.4465], σ=[0.247,0.243,0.261])', 'hardware: A10G GPU (3.6× cheaper than H100, same wall-time for this scale)', 'inference_budget_cap: matched at ≈5× single-forward-pass FLOPs per unlabeled image across both methods (K=5 ensemble members ≈ T=20 MC passes with p≈0.5 effective path fraction)']
- baseline: Single deterministic ResNet-8 trained with standard cross-entropy on the labeled split; pseudo-label confidence scored by Maximum Softmax Probability (MSP, Hendrycks & Gimpel 2017). No dropout at inference. This is the canonical, well-documented SSL pseudo-labeling baseline and is built and validated FIRST before either experimental arm.
- eval contract: Dataset: CIFAR-10 standard test set (10 k images). Primary metric: Area Under the Pseudo-Label Precision–Coverage curve (AUPPC), computed over the 20-point threshold sweep per (method × labels_per_class) cell. Secondary metric: downstream test accuracy after one pseudo-label round at the threshold that maximizes F1 of pseudo-label precision × coverage. Statistical test: Wilcoxon signed-rank test (paired, non-parametric) across 5 random labeled splits per cell; Holm–Bonferroni correction applied across the 2 labels_per_class conditions (α=0.05 family-wise). CV scheme: 5 independent stratified labeled splits; no data leakage between labeled val (used for nuisance tuning) and unlabeled pool (used for pseudo-labeling).
