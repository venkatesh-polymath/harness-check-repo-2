## Idea
Forward-Variance Saturation Score Predicts Layer-Wise Training-Time Saturation Order in Tanh MLPs

## Prediction (registered before running)
Define per-layer initialization-time saturation score S_l = P(|z_l^(0)| > c) where c≈2.0 (where tanh' < 0.1). Under standard Glorot init (n·σ²≈1), tanh compresses variance layer by layer: Var(z_{l+1}) = n·σ²·E[tanh(z_l)²] ≤ Var(z_l) because E[tanh(x)²] < Var(x) for Gaussian x with Var(x)>1. So S_l decreases monotonically with depth at init, making layer 1 most saturated and the first to deepen into full saturation during training. Under super-standard init (n·σ² > 1), variance grows with depth, reversing S_l's ordering and predicting that the deepest layer saturates first. Expected finding: Spearman rank correlation between {S_l at init} and {training epoch at which layer l crosses 80% saturation fraction} exceeds 0.9 across all (width, init-scale) configurations tested. Initial evidence: Glorot & Bengio Fig 6/7 shows layer 1 saturates first under standard init; the variance compression argument follows directly from tanh's bounded output; the ordering-reversal under super-standard init is a specific, immediately testable non-obvious consequence.

## Study spec
- goal: Determine whether the Forward-Variance Saturation Score S_l computed at initialization predicts the layer-wise saturation-crossing order during training in tanh MLPs, and specifically whether this ordering reverses (deepest-layer-first) in the super-standard init regime (n·σ²>1) relative to a depth-index null model — distinguishing the mechanistic claim from a trivial depth-order restatement
- scientific (measure): ['init_regime: {sub-standard: n·σ²=0.5, standard: n·σ²=1.0 (Glorot), super-standard-2x: n·σ²=2.0, super-standard-4x: n·σ²=4.0} — the causal manipulation under test; regimes are defined by the product of fan-in and per-layer σ², set exactly before training', 'network_depth: {3, 5, 7, 9, 12} — varied to provide ≥5 within-config rank-correlation degrees of freedom; 9- and 12-layer nets are required for the reversal to have enough ranks to be non-degenerate', 'S_l_at_init: forward-variance saturation score per layer, computed analytically from init weights before any gradient step — the predictor whose Spearman rank correlation with crossing-epoch is the primary estimand']
- nuisance (tune per arm): ['learning_rate: grid-searched per (init_regime, depth) arm over {1e-3, 3e-3, 1e-2}; chosen as the highest value that keeps loss finite through epoch 5; reported per arm so comparisons are fair-convergence-controlled', 'per_layer_σ²: set per arm to achieve target n·σ² exactly given fan-in; logged to three decimal places for reproducibility', 'random_seed: 5 pre-registered seeds {0,1,2,3,4} per (init_regime, depth) config; used only for CI estimation on Spearman, not as a swept variable']
- fixed (hold constant): ['dataset: MNIST 60k/10k split — fixed; caveat: domain-specific claim, generalization to other datasets not asserted', "architecture: fully-connected tanh MLP, no batch-norm, no skip connections, no dropout — caveat: any forward-variance-altering module (LayerNorm, ResNet) invalidates S_l's analytical form and is explicitly out of scope", 'hidden_width: 256 units per layer — fixed to isolate depth and init effects; caveat: width-regime interactions not tested', 'optimizer: SGD with momentum=0.9, no weight decay — fixed; caveat: adaptive optimizers alter effective curvature and may decouple saturation dynamics from initialization', 'batch_size: 256 — fixed', 'max_epochs: 200 — fixed; configs that never reach the saturation threshold are right-censored at epoch 200 and reported as a separate failure-mode count, not imputed or included in Spearman', 'saturation_unit_threshold: |tanh(pre-activation)| > 0.99 — pre-registered; caveat: threshold choice shifts absolute crossing-epoch values but should not affect rank order', "saturation_layer_threshold: ≥80% of units in a layer exceeding the unit threshold constitutes a 'crossing' — pre-registered before any data collection", 'logging_frequency: every epoch — mandatory to minimise crossing-epoch quantization ties that distort Spearman; every-10-epoch logging is explicitly prohibited', 'divergence_rule: loss NaN or >1e4 after 5 epochs → arm excluded and counted in a divergence table; super-standard divergences are a named failure mode distinct from the reversal test', 'loss: categorical cross-entropy with softmax output', 'input_normalization: pixel values divided by 255 to [0,1] — fixed']
- baseline: Standard Glorot initialization (n·σ²=1.0) across all five depths {3,5,7,9,12} with all five seeds, run FIRST and fully analyzed before any other regime is touched. This arm serves four gatekeeping functions: (1) reproduce the Glorot & Bengio 2010 observation that shallower layers saturate last, confirming the phenomenon is present in this exact setup; (2) establish the depth-index null model's Spearman ceiling (trivially high because S_l and layer-index are co-monotone here) — this ceiling is the bar every other regime must beat or invert; (3) validate all four hypothesis-chain prerequisites (byte-identical loss across seeds, init loss ≈ ln(10) ≈ 2.303, zero-input loss constancy under SGD, single-batch overfit to near-zero loss); (4) confirm no divergence or right-censoring occurs under standard init, providing a clean anchor for the right-censoring analysis in super-standard regimes. No super-standard or sub-standard arm is analyzed until this baseline passes all four checks.
- eval contract: Dataset: MNIST only. Primary metric: Spearman rank correlation ρ(S_l at init, crossing-epoch) computed within each (init_regime, depth) config across its L layers, then averaged over 5 seeds with 95% bootstrap CIs (10,000 resamples per config). Pre-registered primary test: across all super-standard configs (n·σ²>1), ρ(S_l) is significantly more negative than ρ(depth_index_null) — a one-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on ΔSpearman = ρ(S_l) − ρ(depth_index) pooled over super-standard (init_regime, depth) pairs, α=0.05, Bonferroni-corrected for the two super-standard regimes (2x, 4x). Secondary test: mixed-effects linear regression on all non-censored (config, layer) pairs with crossing-epoch as outcome, S_l as fixed predictor, per-config random intercept and slope; report fixed-effect β and 95% CI as a regime-pooled continuous validation. Depth-index null model (predictor = layer index) is the formal comparator in every regime: ΔSpearman must be significantly >0 in sub-standard and standard (S_l adds signal beyond trivial depth order) and significantly <0 in super-standard (reversal). Right-censoring rate and divergence rate are reported as secondary outcomes per regime. Effect size is Cohen's d on ΔSpearman across seeds.
