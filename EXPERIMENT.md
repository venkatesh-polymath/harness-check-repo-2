## Idea
Spectral Null-Space Re-initialization (SNRI): A Provably Non-Disruptive, Rank-Maximizing Plasticity Maintenance Rule

## Prediction (registered before running)
On CIFAR-100 class-incremental: SNRI maintains effective rank ≥20% higher than continual backprop at the same re-init budget (same fraction of neurons re-initialized per task), and shows ≤5% additional catastrophic forgetting versus the no-re-init baseline (compared to ~15% additional forgetting for continual backprop's non-orthogonal random re-init). SNRI's advantage increases with network depth because deeper networks have more layers where null-space capacity is available for targeted injection. The depth-sensitivity separates SNRI from SWR, which has no depth-dependent rank-injection mechanism. Outgoing-weight zero-start slows initial reactivation but the optimizer reliably escapes within 1–2 tasks, a cost visible in the training curve but not the asymptotic performance.

## Study spec
- goal: Measure whether SNRI maintains higher effective rank of weight matrices throughout training versus no-plasticity-fix and existing fixes (ReDo, Shrink-and-Perturb, L2-init), without degrading final top-1 accuracy on CIFAR-100.
- scientific (measure): ['plasticity_rule: {none, SNRI, ReDo, shrink_and_perturb, L2_init}', 'snri_null_space_threshold_epsilon: float — singular-value cutoff below which a direction is treated as null-space', 'snri_reinit_frequency: int — gradient steps between successive null-space re-initialization passes']
- nuisance (tune per arm): ['learning_rate: grid-searched independently per plasticity_rule arm', 'weight_decay: tuned independently per arm', 'perturbation_scale_or_reset_fraction: tuned per competing method (shrink-and-perturb noise scale; ReDo dead-unit reset fraction; L2-init pull strength)']
- fixed (hold constant): ['architecture: ResNet-18 (no pre-training)', 'dataset: CIFAR-100, standard 50 k train / 10 k test split', 'optimizer: SGD, momentum=0.9', 'batch_size: 128', 'training_epochs: 200', 'lr_schedule: cosine annealing to 0', 'data_augmentation: random 32x32 crop + horizontal flip + channel-wise normalize (standard CIFAR protocol)', 'random_seeds: [0,1,2,3,4] — identical across all arms', 'hardware: single NVIDIA A100; caveat: bit-identical reproducibility not guaranteed across GPU generations']
- baseline: Vanilla ResNet-18 + SGD + cosine LR on CIFAR-100 with zero plasticity intervention — the published continual-learning plasticity benchmark with documented effective-rank collapse and accuracy reference numbers (Lyle et al. 2023, Abbas et al. 2023).
- eval contract: Dataset: CIFAR-100 fixed standard split. Primary metric: effective rank (nuclear-norm / Frobenius-norm ratio) per weight matrix, sampled every 10 epochs, reported as AUC over the full training curve averaged across layers. Secondary metrics: final top-1 test accuracy, dead-neuron fraction (ReLU units with zero activation on entire val set at epoch 200). Sanity gates before any arm runs: (1) loss at init ≈ log(100) = 4.605 ± 0.01, (2) constant-predictor accuracy = 1.0 ± 0.1%, (3) single-batch memorization drives training loss < 0.01 within 500 steps, (4) two runs with identical seed produce bit-identical final weight checksums. Statistical test: paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test across 5 seeds (α = 0.05, Bonferroni-corrected for 4 pairwise comparisons vs. baseline) on both effective-rank AUC and final accuracy; report effect size (rank-biserial r).
