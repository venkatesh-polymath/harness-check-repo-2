## Idea
BatchNorm's Implicit Regularization Is Weight Decay for Grokking: A Normalization-Decomposition Experiment

## Prediction (registered before running)
In a 2×2 experiment (norm ∈ {BN, LN}) × (WD ∈ {0, matched-value}): (a) LN + no WD will never grok within budget; (b) BN + no WD will grok at a characteristic delay; (c) LN + matched WD will grok at nearly the same delay as (b); (d) BN + matched WD will grok fastest. This pattern confirms BN's regularization benefit in delayed generalization is entirely mediated by implicit L2, not by optimization smoothing. A mismatch between (b) and (c) would instead point to BN's non-regularization effects (gradient noise, landscape smoothing) as the active mechanism — an equally informative result.
