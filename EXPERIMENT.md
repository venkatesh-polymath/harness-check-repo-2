## Idea
BatchNorm's Implicit Regularization Is Weight Decay for Grokking: A Normalization-Decomposition Experiment

## Prediction (registered before running)
In a 2×2 experiment (norm ∈ {BN, LN}) × (WD ∈ {0, matched-value}): (a) LN + no WD will never grok within budget; (b) BN + no WD will grok at a characteristic delay; (c) LN + matched WD will grok at nearly the same delay as (b); (d) BN + matched WD will grok fastest. This pattern confirms BN's regularization benefit in delayed generalization is entirely mediated by implicit L2, not by optimization smoothing. A mismatch between (b) and (c) would instead point to BN's non-regularization effects (gradient noise, landscape smoothing) as the active mechanism — an equally informative result.

- goal: Test whether grokking delay and weight-norm trajectory under BN+no-WD are statistically consistent with LN+WD when WD is calibrated via fitted equivalence (not numerically matched), across a sweep of batch sizes that explicitly controls BN's noise-injection magnitude — tempered to a consistency/decomposition claim, not a mechanistic-totality claim.
- scientific (measure): ['norm_type ∈ {None, LayerNorm, BatchNorm} — primary treatment axis; determines regularization source, gradient geometry, and scale-invariance properties', "batch_size ∈ {32, 128, 512, full-batch (9409)} — explicit scientific variable; BN's implicit regularization scales ∝ 1/batch_size so this is the single most important knob for the hypothesis and must be swept, not left implicit", 'weight_decay ∈ {0, 1e-4, 3e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3, 1e-2} — swept across LN arms as a scientific axis; for BN arms, WD is set by calibration (see nuisance), not by matching the scalar numerically']

- fixed (hold constant): ['task: modular addition p=97, labels=(a+b) mod 97, vocab_size=98 (97 residues + separator token); caveat: delay-equivalence result may not transfer to other grokking tasks or primes', 'train_fraction: 0.4 of all p²=9409 pairs (3763 train / 5646 val), stratified by label; changing this fraction shifts the memorization-generalization boundary and must not vary across cells', 'architecture: 1-layer transformer, d_model=128, n_heads=4, MLP_hidden=512, learned token+position embeddings, no dropout; BN placed pre-MLP-activation (post-embedding); caveat: BN placement is fixed — pre- vs post-activation placement is a confound if varied', 'optimizer: AdamW, β1=0.9, β2=0.98, ε=1e-8; WD mask excludes normalization parameters and embeddings (consistent with standard practice); gradient clipping disabled', 'max_training_steps: 100,000; evaluation every 100 steps on full validation set', 'n_seeds: 5 independent seeds per cell, each seed controlling initialization + data shuffle + mini-batch ordering via torch.manual_seed(seed) before all stochastic ops for bit-for-bit reproducibility', "grokking_delay definition: first step t such that val_acc(t) ≥ 0.95 AND val_acc(t') ≥ 0.95 for all t' ∈ [t, t+500]; cells with no grokking by step 100k are right-censored at 100,000", 'pilot_gate: BN+no-WD+batch_size=512 must confirm val_acc ≥ 0.95 within 100k steps across ≥3 of 5 seeds before committing to full factorial sweep; if pilot fails, halt and revise premise (b)']
