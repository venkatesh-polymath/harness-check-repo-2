## Idea
Adam's v_t is the Fisher — repurposing optimizer state as a zero-overhead neuron utility for plasticity-preserving resets

## Prediction (registered before running)
Adam-CBP (utility = max v_t over outgoing weights per neuron) will match explicit Fisher-CBP on final-task accuracy and effective rank within noise margin, while being 8–12× faster per reset step (no extra forward/backward pass). SGD + explicit Fisher will perform comparably to Adam-CBP, confirming that accumulation over time (not just current-task snapshot) is the key property. SGD-CBP with heuristic will underperform both, isolating the utility criterion as the decisive factor rather than the optimizer choice. Initial evidence: v_t = E[g²] ≈ diag(F) is a mathematical identity, not a hypothesis; Adam's superior plasticity retention over SGD in continual settings has been independently reported in multiple ablations without a mechanistic account — this is the missing explanation.

## Study spec
- goal: Determine whether Adam's accumulated exp_avg_sq (v_t, an empirical second-moment EMA) is a statistically equivalent substitute for an explicit empirical-Fisher per-neuron utility in CBP resets — and whether temporal accumulation rather than optimizer identity is the mechanistically decisive property
- scientific (measure): ['utility_source × optimizer arm: (A) Adam + no-reset [baseline], (B) Adam + CBP + magnitude-heuristic, (C) Adam + CBP + explicit-empirical-Fisher [separate gradient pass over observed labels], (D) Adam + CBP + v_t-EMA [zero-overhead proxy], (E) Adam + CBP + snapshot-g² [single current-batch squared gradient, no accumulation], (F) SGD + CBP + explicit-empirical-Fisher [isolates optimizer from utility]', "accumulation_window: EMA (β₂-decayed history) vs single-batch snapshot — varied only between arms D and E to license the 'accumulation is decisive' sub-claim"]
- nuisance (tune per arm): ['learning_rate: grid-searched independently per arm on held-out task-1 validation accuracy (same log-spaced grid, same compute budget per arm)', 'reset_fraction p: tuned per arm on plasticity proxy (effective rank recovery after task switch) using task-2 validation', 'reset_interval T (steps between reset sweeps): tuned per arm within [50, 500]', 'Adam β₁, β₂ / SGD momentum: tuned per arm within standard documented ranges; β₂ also controls accumulation window depth in arm D']
- fixed (hold constant): ['architecture: ResNet-18, standard torchvision implementation — fixed across all arms; no architecture search', 'dataset_sequence: 5-task split-CIFAR-100 (20 classes/task, fixed class order, rng_seed=42 for class assignment)', 'batch_size: 256', 'steps_per_task: 2 000', 'num_seeds: 5 (seeds 0–4; all arms × all seeds, fully crossed)', 'Fisher variant in arms C and F: empirical Fisher only (CE gradient on observed labels, NOT model-sampled targets) — documented explicitly to prevent trivial algebraic match with v_t near the optimum from inflating the equivalence result', 'equivalence margin: ±1.0 pp absolute mean final-task top-1 accuracy, pre-registered before any arm is unblinded', 'speedup metric definition: wall-clock seconds per reset step on a single H100, averaged over 100 reset events, mean ± std — arm D vs arm C; reported separately, not folded into the equivalence test', 'hypothesis-chain sanity suite (must all pass before main arms run): (i) two runs with identical seed produce bit-identical per-step loss; (ii) random-init CE loss ∈ [log(10)−0.05, log(10)+0.05] nats; (iii) permuted-label CE does not fall below log(10)−0.10 after 10 epochs; (iv) single batch of 64 CIFAR-10 samples overfits to CE < 0.01 within 500 steps', 'caveat — generality: all claims scoped to split-CIFAR-100 on ResNet-18; out-of-distribution generalization (other architectures, longer sequences, different Fisher variants) left to follow-up']
- baseline: Arm A — Adam + no resets (vanilla Adam continual fine-tuning, tasks presented sequentially): the most widely documented, zero-design-choice reference in plasticity literature; build and validate first to confirm forgetting occurs and effective rank decays, establishing the phenomenon the CBP arms are meant to fix before any utility comparison is made
- eval contract: Dataset: 5-task split-CIFAR-100, ResNet-18 pretrained on CIFAR-10. Primary metric: mean final-task top-1 accuracy averaged over tasks 2–5 (task 1 excluded as warm-up). Secondary metric: effective rank of the final conv layer weight matrix at end of each task (rank collapse as plasticity proxy). Equivalence test (utility-source claim): two one-sided paired t-tests (TOST, α=0.05, Δ=1.0 pp) on primary metric between arm D (v_t-EMA) and arm C (explicit-Fisher) across 5 seeds — both one-sided p-values must be < 0.05 to declare equivalence. Accumulation test (mechanistic claim): TOST (same α, Δ) between arm D (v_t-EMA) and arm E (snapshot-g²); a significant difference (i.e., TOST fails to find equivalence) licenses 'accumulation is the decisive property.' Optimizer-isolation test: paired t-test (α=0.05, two-sided) between arm C (Adam+Fisher) and arm F (SGD+Fisher) — tests whether optimizer identity beyond utility source affects outcome. Speedup reported as mean ± std wall-clock seconds/reset-step (arm D vs arm C), not folded into accuracy equivalence.
