## Idea
Online Mid-Training Invariance Auditor via Unlabeled Feature-Tail Consistency Signal

## Prediction (registered before running)
FixMatch with the online invariance auditor will show lower CIFAR-10-C mean corruption error than FixMatch+RandAugment by 2–4%, with auditor rollbacks correlating with known harmful operations (heavy contrast, saturation) and triggering more frequently at lower label budgets where the model is more corruption-sensitive.

## Study spec
- goal: Measure whether an online unlabeled feature-tail consistency signal detects mid-training invariance drift earlier and more reliably than validation-loss monitoring, quantified by AUROC of the auditor signal against the held-out train-val generalisation gap across training steps.
- scientific (measure): ['auditor_enabled ∈ {False (baseline), True} — primary treatment variable', "feature_tail_percentile_threshold τ ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.20} — defines which unlabeled samples constitute the 'tail'", 'consistency_metric ∈ {prediction_entropy_variance, feature_space_MMD, output_KL_divergence} — the signal used to measure invariance drift']
- nuisance (tune per arm): ['learning_rate: tuned independently per arm via grid search on a dedicated 5k val split (ensures no arm is penalised by a suboptimal lr)', 'weight_decay: tuned per arm over {1e-4, 5e-4, 1e-3}', 'unlabeled_audit_batch_size: tuned per auditor-active arm so auditor wall-clock overhead ≤5% of total step time']
- fixed (hold constant): ['architecture: ResNet-8 (caveat: findings scoped to shallow CNNs; may not transfer to deeper or attention-based models)', 'dataset: CIFAR-10 with canonical 50k/10k train/test split (caveat: balanced 10-class; behaviour under long-tail or covariate-shift distributions untested)', 'optimizer: SGD with momentum=0.9', 'labeled_batch_size: 64', 'training_epochs: 200', 'global_random_seed: single fixed seed verified for bit-for-bit reproducibility per hypothesis-chain check 1', 'hardware: single NVIDIA A10G GPU (IS_SANDBOX=1 for root pods per harness config)', 'feature-tail construction: k-means on CIFAR-10 training embeddings; tail = bottom-τ density samples held out as unlabeled audit pool']
- baseline: Vanilla ResNet-8 on CIFAR-10 with SGD, no auditor, tuned lr and weight_decay — built first and validated against all four hypothesis-chain checks: (1) bit-for-bit loss-curve reproducibility under fixed seed, (2) pre-gradient CE loss within 1% of log(10)≈2.3026, (3) uniform-predictor test accuracy = 10.0%±0.1%, (4) single-batch memorisation to ≤0.01 CE within 500 SGD steps at lr=0.01.
- eval contract: Dataset: CIFAR-10 (50k train, 10k test) with a 10% unlabeled feature-tail audit pool held out from training. Primary metric: final test accuracy. Secondary metric: AUROC of the per-step auditor consistency signal as a predictor of the instantaneous train-val generalisation gap. Statistical test: paired t-test across 5 fixed random seeds comparing each auditor-active arm against the no-auditor baseline; α=0.05 with Bonferroni correction over the 3 consistency-metric variants (adjusted threshold α′=0.0167); effect size reported as Cohen's d.
